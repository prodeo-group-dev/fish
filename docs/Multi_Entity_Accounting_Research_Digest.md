## 1. Multi-currency in a general ledger (researcher 1)

### 1.1 IAS 21 in plain terms
- Functional currency is the currency of the entity's main economic environment; the presentation currency is the entity's free choice. [P: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/]
- Initial recognition is at the spot rate on the transaction date; an average rate for a week or month is allowed only where rates do not move much (para 22, unconfirmed); seasonality needs a transaction-by-transaction or weighted basis. [S: https://www.icaew.com/technical/corporate-reporting/ifrs/ifrs-accounting-standards-tracker/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates; HMRC's manual says the same, P: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/corporate-finance-manual/cfm64170]
- Monetary items (cash, receivables, payables) are retranslated at the closing rate; non-monetary items at historical cost keep the transaction-date rate; non-monetary items at fair value use the rate on the fair-value date; differences on monetary items go to profit or loss. [S: CPA Ireland summary (via search snippet) https://www.cpaireland.ie/CPAIreland/media/Education-Training/Study%20Support%20Resources/F2%20Financial%20Accounting/Relevant%20Articles/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates.pdf ; https://www.bdo.com.au/en-au/blogs/corporate-reporting/accounting-for-foreign-exchange-transactions-in-a-non-hyperinflationary-economy]
- "Realised" and "unrealised" are system labels, not IAS 21 terms: realised arises at settlement, unrealised on retranslating open items at period end; both go to profit or loss. The labels are used in the vendor documentation in 1.2.
- A monetary item forming part of the net investment in a foreign operation: profit or loss in the entity's own statements, OCI in the consolidated statements. [S: same sources]
- Translating a foreign operation into a presentation currency: assets and liabilities at the closing rate; income and expenses at transaction-date rates (averages used in practice); all differences to OCI, accumulated in a separate equity component (the translation reserve). [S: CPA Ireland snippet]
- On disposal the cumulative translation amount is reclassified to profit or loss (para 48, unconfirmed); partial disposals reclassify a proportionate share; the share attributable to non-controlling interests is derecognised but not recycled. [P: IFRS staff paper https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/meetings/2010/march/ifric/1003ap7dobs-aip-ias-21-cta-recycling-in-ias-27r-transactions.pdf ; IFRIC 2020 agenda decision]
- IAS 29 (hyperinflation): statements are restated into the measuring unit current at period end, with a net monetary position gain or loss in profit or loss. [P: https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2024/issued/ias29.html (via search summary)] PwC's June 2026 list names nine economies (Argentina, Haiti, Iran, Lebanon, Malawi, South Sudan, Sudan, Turkey, Venezuela), says Zimbabwe's status is unclear, and says Sierra Leone ceased to be hyperinflationary by 31 March 2026. [S: https://www.pwc.ch/en/insights/accounting/hyper-inflationary-economies-june-2026.html (via search; the page returned 403)] EY lists Nigeria as not hyperinflationary but to be monitored. [S: https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/technical/ifrs-technical-resources/documents/ey-gl-ifrs-developments-249-hyperinflationary-economies-04-2026.pdf] The 2023 "lack of exchangeability" amendment is also relevant. [P: IFRS page above]

### 1.2 How mainstream systems store and process it
- **Dynamics 365 Finance** [P: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/configure-ledger ; .../foreign-currency-revaluation-general-ledger]: each voucher line holds the transaction-currency amount, the accounting-currency amount and an optional reporting-currency amount; the ledger sets separate rate types for accounting, reporting and budget; rate types are defined per currency pair; revaluation can use a different rate type per main account; separate ledger accounts exist for realised gain, realised loss, unrealised gain and unrealised loss; GL revaluation posts only the delta against the prior balance and does not auto-reverse, while AR and AP revaluation reverse and re-post; a reversal creates a new history record.
- **SAP** [P: https://learning.sap.com/courses/configuring-the-financial-closing-in-sap-s-4hana/managing-the-foreign-currency-valuation ; currency types via SAP Learning search snippet]: currency types are document (00), company code (10), controlling area (20), group (30), plus hard and index-based; rate types are assigned per currency type; valuation posts the difference to an adjustment account and reverses automatically on the first day of the next period; delta logic avoids the reversal; the realised difference is posted at clearing. Rates sit in TCURR with a direct or indirect quotation setting [S: forum threads only; lower confidence].
- **Oracle Fusion GL** [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/25d/faigl/guidelines-for-creating-conversion-rate-types.html]: rate types Spot, Corporate, User and Fixed; the same pair can have different rates per type for the same date; period-average and period-end rate types drive translation; reporting currencies and secondary ledgers exist at balance level or journal level. The page does not say how entered and accounted amounts are stored (not confirmed).
- **NetSuite** [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1430227.html ; .../section_N1405625.html]: month-end revaluation uses last-day rates and posts the total variance to Unrealised Gain/Loss; realised gain or loss posts automatically when a payment is applied; consolidated rates are stored per subsidiary pair, per period and per book as Current, Average or Historical (Current for balance-sheet items, Average for the income statement, Historical for equity); the balancing line is the CTA.
- **Odoo** [P: https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/account/models/account_move_line.py ; https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/get_started/multi_currency.html]: lines store `amount_currency`, `currency_id` and `balance` (company currency), with `debit` and `credit` derived from `balance`; the rate is a non-stored computed field (for invoices it comes from the move's invoice rate); exchange differences go to a dedicated journal and dedicated gain and loss accounts; rates can be entered manually or fetched on a schedule. No official core documentation on revaluation was found.
- **Xero** [P via search snippets only; central.xero.com did not render for the fetch tool: https://central.xero.com/0/article/Foreign-currency-accounts-in-the-chart-of-accounts-GL]: unrealised amounts are computed on open items; realised amounts arise at settlement; revalued foreign bank accounts post to a dedicated chart account. A 2013 community thread says rates were synced from xe.com every 6 hours (old, unconfirmed). [S: https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/175380]
- **QuickBooks Online** [P: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/getting-started/frequently-asked-questions-about-home-currency-adjustments/00/263191]: a "home currency adjustment" journal records the date, currency and rate; unrealised AR/AP gain or loss is computed in reports and is **not** in the GL or trial balance; bank adjustments are realised; the rate source is not stated. A revaluation entry's date, amounts and rate cannot be edited; you delete and rerun.
- **NetSuite reopening a period:** rerunning revaluation in a reopened period means rerunning every later closed period. [P: NetSuite links above]
- **Common pattern.** Every product keeps the original-currency amount and the base-currency amount on the line, and the rate is either stored or derivable. They differ on four points: how many parallel currencies the ledger carries (SAP, Oracle and D365 carry several; Odoo, Xero and QuickBooks carry one base); whether unrealised amounts are posted to the GL (SAP, D365, NetSuite and Odoo-with-add-on do; Xero and QuickBooks compute them in reports); whether the period-end revaluation auto-reverses (SAP by default, D365 GL no, NetSuite's page does not say); and whether there are rate types (SAP, Oracle and D365 have them; NetSuite uses three consolidation types).

### 1.3 Pitfalls
- **Rounding.** Converting each line separately can leave an entry out of balance by a few units of the smallest currency unit; vendor guidance is to post the difference automatically to a per-currency rounding account, separate from realised FX, with a threshold so real errors are not absorbed. [S: Visma https://docs.vismasoftware.no/visma-net-erp/help/currency-management/configure-currency/about-currency-rounding-rules/ ; Infor SunSystems; Sage 50 guide (via search)]
- Backdated rates, rate-source audit trail, mixed-currency report totals and foreign bank reconciliation: **no source found**; what follows in the playbook on these is inference.

### 1.4 Exchange-rate sources
- **Bank of England** [P: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/Rates.asp?TD=8]: 26 currencies including USD and EUR; **NGN and SLE are not listed**; the page states the rates are "not official rates". Download in CSV and XML (`_iadb-fromshowcolumns.asp`, series codes, a 300-series limit). [P: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/help.asp] Reuse: the database is under the Open Government Licence except some exchange-rate series excluded for third-party (LSEG) licences, where permission is needed. [P: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/legal]

Reuse is free with accurate reproduction, ECB cited, any modification stated. [P: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/disclaimer/html/index.en.html]
- **Central Bank of Nigeria:** the rates page gives the NFEM rate in naira per US dollar only (high, low, close, average) with an "Export to Excel" button, and says the NFEM rate "stands as the official exchange rate for the day". [P: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/rates/ExchRateByCurrency.html] No official API or licence terms found; third-party wrappers exist. [S: https://allratestoday.com/central-bank-rates-api/cbn/]
- **Bank of Sierra Leone:** publishes daily "Indicative Exchange Rates" as PDFs with a weighted mid-rate sourced from Refinitiv, WAMA and IMF. [P: bsl.gov.sl "Indicative Exchange Rates - 10-07-2025.pdf", found via search] A page `bsl.gov.sl/WAMZ_Exchange_Rates.html` is cited by a third party, and only the last few weeks are said to stay online. [S: https://allratestoday.com/central-bank-rates-api/bsl/] Some file names do not match the dates printed inside the sheets. No machine-readable feed or licence terms found.
- **Nigeria, official versus parallel.** Daily news reports for October 2026 show a gap of roughly 24 to 57 naira per dollar over the summer, with parallel quotes varying by outlet. [S: https://techeconomy.ng/dollar-naira-gap-widens-as-parallel-market-quotes-1-at-1385] IMF material, as reported by BusinessDay, puts the premium at about 30% before the June 2023 unification, peaks over 70%, and about 9% on average after. [S: https://businessday.ng/news/article/imf-says-nigerias-fx-reforms-revived-monetary-policy-as-floating-naira-reshapes-inflation/] A bookkeeping system should record the rate used, which rate series it came from and the date; whether to record the parallel rate is a policy call for Femi. No authoritative source reconciles the gap figures.

### 1.5 A person employed in one country but taxed under another's payroll tax (structure options only)
- A foreign subsidiary becomes the local employer with local payroll (heaviest option). [S: https://www.rippling.com/glossary/foreign-subsidiary]
- A branch is part of the parent; a permanent establishment is a tax outcome, not a structure one chooses, and it can arise from a single worker; an employer of record becomes the legal employer but does not remove PE risk. [S: https://www.paychex.com/glossary/what-is-permanent-establishment-risk ; https://hellopebl.com/resources/blog/creating-a-foreign-branch-vs-subsidiary-which-one-is-right-for-your-business/]
- UK-specific: an overseas employer with no UK presence may voluntarily run PAYE; under section 689 ITEPA 2003 a UK entity for which the employee works may have to run PAYE as if it were the employer, depending on the facts. [P: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-manual/paye81511]
- Secondary-level pointers; the tax conclusions are Femi's.

### 1.6 Researcher 1's minimal design (inference, not sourced)
Store per line: transaction currency, transaction amount, base amount, and the rate with its source, date and type. Keep a rate table of (pair, date, rate, source, entered-by) with a flagged manual override. Compute base amounts at the transaction-date spot rate, the realised difference at settlement to a dedicated account, period-end retranslation of monetary items as dated entries (with a switch for reversal), and a per-currency rounding line. Leave out of version one: presentation-currency translation, consolidation and CTA, IAS 29 restatement, hedging and net-investment designation, triangulation and rate-type tables, multiple parallel currencies. Reports must group by currency unless they use base amounts.

## 2. Departmental and branch accounting (researcher 2)

### 2.1 Departmental accounting as taught
- Responsibility centres: a cost-centre manager answers for spend only; a profit centre is judged on revenue less costs; an investment centre on profit relative to capital; the organising principle is controllability. [S: https://courses.lumenlearning.com/wm-managerialaccounting/chapter/introduction-to-responsibility-centers/ ; https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/responsibility-accounting-explained-centres-guide/]
- Departmental accounts are a separate trading and profit and loss account per department; common costs are apportioned on usual bases: floor area (rent, rates, heat and light), asset cost or net book value (depreciation, insurance), headcount (canteen, personnel). [S: https://www.acowtancy.com/textbook/cima-ba2/b2-apply-absorption-costing-and-marginal-costing/allocation-and-apportionment/notes ; https://www.savemyexams.com/as/accounting/cie/21/revision-notes/cost-and-management-accounting/absorption-costing/allocation-and-apportionment-of-overheads]
- Service-department methods: **direct** (ignores service between support departments; simplest, least accurate), **step-down** (one-way, needs a ranking of support departments), **reciprocal** (simultaneous equations, most accurate). [S: https://iastate.pressbooks.pub/isudp-2025-202/?p=216 ; https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/cch-tagetik/glossary/cost-allocation]
- Departmental balance sheet content: **no source found**.

### 2.2 How ERPs model departments
- **Oracle** [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/26a/faigl/chart-of-accounts-components.html]: segments are components of an account combination; the primary balancing segment is required and ensures journals balance for each balancing-segment value, which supports trial-balance reporting; Cost Center is an optional label (required for Oracle Assets and for expense approval limits); the Intercompany label drives intercompany balancing.
- **Dynamics 365** [P: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/default-dimensions ; .../set-up-balance-dimensions (search summary only)]: account structures hold the main account plus up to 10 dimensions and define which combinations are valid and whether a value is mandatory; a ledger can name one balancing dimension and the system auto-posts balancing entries so each value balances; main accounts can carry fixed or default dimension values.
- **NetSuite** [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N261486.html]: Department, Class and Location are built-in classifications, custom segments unlimited; in OneWorld each must be tied to a subsidiary; the page does not cover mandatory settings or header versus line (a third-party guide says departments and classes can be made mandatory, S).
- **Odoo** [P: https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/applications/finance/accounting/reporting/analytic_accounting.html]: analytic plans group analytic accounts and can be nested; each plan is Optional, Mandatory or Unavailable; Mandatory blocks confirming an entry without a distribution; applicability lines can override per account prefix or product category.
- **SAP** [S: SAP Learning snippets https://learning.sap.com/courses/profit-center-accounting-in-sap-s-4hana/assigning-profit-centers-to-account-assignment-objects ; https://help.sap.com/doc/3c51d7531a4d424de10000000a174cb4/2.6/en-US/4911c9cc2a934a18e10000000a42189b.html]: a profit centre is normally derived from cost centres or orders, not typed; document splitting gives a balance sheet by profit centre or segment; segment sits above profit centre; a zero-balance setting forces each splitting characteristic to balance within a document.
- **Xero** [P: https://central.xero.com/0/article/Set-up-tracking-categories ; .../Tracking-Summary-Report ; .../Profit-and-Loss-New]: at most four tracking categories in total, only two active at once; the Tracking Summary shows untracked amounts as "Unassigned"; the profit and loss compares options in columns; the Balance Sheet article does not mention tracking.
- **QuickBooks Online** [P via search summary: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/pay-bills/balance-sheet-class-report-unclassified-amounts/L21r11TfH_US_en_US]: the balance sheet uses transaction headers and the profit and loss uses lines; Class attaches to lines only, Location to header and lines, so there is no true balance sheet by Class; a class column shows A/R and A/P under "Not specified". Intuit's own pages conflict on a balance sheet by class (section 5).
- **Three storage shapes** (researcher's synthesis): (1) **tags on lines** (Xero, QuickBooks, NetSuite, Odoo): cheap and flexible, profit and loss by tag is easy, a balance sheet by tag is not guaranteed; (2) **chart-of-accounts segment with a balancing segment** (Oracle, Dynamics balancing dimension, SAP splitting): journals balance per value, a full balance sheet per value is possible, at the cost of rigidity and generated clearing lines; (3) **separate ledgers or entities** (NetSuite subsidiary, Dynamics legal entity): strongest separation, needs intercompany accounts and eliminations.

### 2.3 Integrity rules
- Mandatory dimension enforced at post or confirm time: Odoo (plan set to Mandatory) and Dynamics (account structure says whether entry is required; posting fails validation). [P]
- Balancing per dimension value (Oracle, Dynamics, SAP) makes dimension totals reconcile to the ledger by construction.
- An "unassigned" bucket: Xero's Tracking Summary and QuickBooks "Not specified". [P]
- Defaulting order matters: in Dynamics fixed dimensions are applied at posting, after rules were evaluated at entry, which caused a validation failure in Microsoft's own example. [P]
- Effective-dated structures and restating on rename or merge: **no source found**; Xero says archiving preserves past reports (S, forum or blog claim).

### 2.4 Branch accounting as taught
- Dependent branch: books kept at head office only, with periodic returns; in the stock-and-debtors method goods are invoiced at a selling price the branch cannot vary. [S: https://www.scribd.com/document/391506455/Branch-Accounts-Notes ; https://sist.sathyabama.ac.in/sist_coursematerial/uploads/SBA1203.pdf]
- Invoice price: goods sent at invoice price include head-office margin; a stock reserve removes the unrealised profit in closing stock and a branch stock adjustment account carries the loading. [S: same]
- Inter-branch accounts: the Head Office account (in branch books) and the Branch account (in head-office books) should show equal and opposite balances; goods in transit and cash in transit are recorded on both sides so they agree; inter-branch transfers are posted as if through head office. [S: prgc.edu.in Advanced Accounting unit notes (URL as printed by the researcher: https://prgc.edu.in/admin/uploads/syllabus/78563rd sem IV Unit Advanced Accounting ppt-2.pdf365.pdf)]
- Foreign branch: IAS 21 defines a foreign operation as a subsidiary, associate, joint arrangement or branch with activities in a different country or currency (from search snippets; the IFRS page is paywalled). Translation: closing rate for the balance sheet, transaction or average rates for the profit and loss, exchange differences to OCI and recycled on disposal. [S: https://ifrscommunity.com/knowledge-base/ias-21-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/]
- **ERPs on branches/intercompany.** Dynamics: intercompany set up as directional legal-entity pairs with due-to and due-from main accounts, a "create reciprocal relationship" button, cross-company settlements auto-generate due-to and due-from, Microsoft suggests unique intercompany accounts per company for reconciliation. [P: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/intercompany-accounting-setup] NetSuite: Advanced Intercompany Journal with Auto Balance between subsidiaries; Automated Intercompany Management generates eliminations at close. [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1475891.html] Oracle: the Intercompany label (2.2). Nothing found on a location or profit-centre dimension used as a branch counterparty.

### 2.5 Reporting standards
- IFRS 8 applies to entities whose debt or equity is publicly traded or that are filing to issue instruments publicly; if a parent presents consolidated and separate statements together, segment data is needed only for the consolidated ones; other entities must not label voluntary data "segment information" unless they comply. [S: https://www.iasplus.com/standard/ifrs08.htm ; https://mlsu.ac.in/econtents/5092_IFRS8.pdf]
- The IFRS for SMEs Standard does not address segment reporting. [S: https://abmagazine.accaglobal.com/global/articles/2022/aug/technical/sme-standards-catch-up.html]
- FRS 102 segment position: **not verified**.

### 2.6 A foreign branch and local tax
A branch is not a separate legal entity; it usually creates a permanent establishment; the VAT "fixed establishment" test differs from the treaty PE test, so VAT registration may be needed even where the PE analysis is unclear. Country-specific commentary only; the conclusion is Femi's. [S: https://www.rsm.hu/en/blog/2023/vat/permanent-establishment-for-tax-purposes ; https://business.gov.nl/running-your-business/legal-forms-and-governance/foreign-entities-and-branches/]

### 2.7 Researcher 2's minimal design (inference, not sourced)
Add a Branch dimension type with its own value list; a per-account rule (dimension required, optional or forbidden: Odoo and Dynamics both have it); a visible "Unassigned" bucket and a check that dimension totals equal the ledger; profit and loss by dimension; an optional department allocation run (direct method, headcount, floor area and revenue bases); archive rather than delete dimension values. Leave out of version one: balancing-segment auto-clearing and a departmental balance sheet, step-down and reciprocal allocation, effective-dated hierarchies and merge/restate, stock-at-invoice-price loading, foreign-branch translation, automatic elimination. Trade-off: **branch as a dimension** (cheap, one ledger, simple consolidated view, but no real branch balance sheet and weaker if a branch needs its own currency, tax registration or books) versus **branch as a Company** (separate ledger, a home for IAS 21 translation and local tax registration, but needs intercompany accounts and eliminations and models a legal-entity boundary a branch does not have; FiSH's Company already means "one General Ledger per legal entity"). Suggested rule: default to a dimension, and use a Company only for a foreign branch with its own currency or tax registration, as a documented exception.

## 3. Consolidated accounts and group reporting (researcher 3)

### 3.1 The IFRS 10 procedure (summary, paragraph numbers unconfirmed)
- Control is the basis; a controlling parent presents the group as one economic entity. [P: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/]
- Procedure, per secondary summaries and the Philippine adoption (B86): combine like items line by line; eliminate the parent's investment against the subsidiary's equity; eliminate intragroup balances, transactions, income and expenses in full; eliminate profit still in inventory or fixed assets. [S: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consolidated_financial_statement ; Landbank PFRS 10 quote] Non-controlling interests shown separately in equity (researcher's knowledge, unsourced).
- Same reporting date; if impracticable, use the subsidiary's latest statements adjusted for significant events in the gap; the gap is at most three months and consistent over time. [S: German text of EU Reg 1126/2008 https://umwelt-online.de/recht/euarchive/08_09/08_1126a_22_09_1/08_1126m.htm]
- Uniform accounting policies: **not retrievable**. Pointers only (unsourced) for IFRS 3 (goodwill), IAS 28 (equity method), IFRS 11 (joint arrangements).

### 3.2 IAS 21 translation for consolidation
- Assets and liabilities at the closing rate; income and expenses at transaction-date rates (an average allowed unless rates fluctuate significantly); differences to OCI, accumulated in a separate equity component (paras 39 and 41, unconfirmed). [P: IFRIC paper https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/meetings/2025/september/ifric/ap2-classification-intragroup-exchange.pdf (read via search snippets); S: https://www.iasplus.com/en/standards/ias/ias21]
- Para 39 does not itself mention equity; the "equity at historical rates" rule is vendor practice: NetSuite [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4685850130.html (via search)] and Odoo [P: https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/finance/accounting/get_started/consolidation.html].
- Intragroup monetary items (para 45): a monetary item cannot be eliminated without showing the FX gain or loss; the loan and related income and expense are eliminated but the exchange difference stays in consolidated profit or loss; IFRIC is still deciding (2026) how to classify that difference under IFRS 18. [P: IFRIC paper above; https://www.ifrs.org/projects/work-plan/classification-foreign-exchange-difference-intragroup-monetary-liability]
- **Sources disagree** (section 5): NetSuite's worked example puts the elimination residual in a CTA-E account.

### 3.3 Who must consolidate (this tells us which customers need it)
- **United Kingdom.** Small-group exemption: a group is small if it meets two of three tests: turnover £15m net or £18m gross, balance sheet £7.5m net or £9m gross, 50 employees; figures substituted for financial years starting on or after 6 April 2025 (SI 2024/1303). [P: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/383] Section 399(2A) exempts a company subject to the small companies regime unless the group contains a listed company, bank or insurer; sections 400 to 402 add further exemptions; voluntary consolidation is allowed. [P: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/399] Section 399 shows no medium-group exemption; medium group figures (section 466) are £54m/£64m turnover, £27m/£32m balance sheet, 250 employees. [P: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/466] ACCA agrees medium and large groups must consolidate. [S: https://www.accaglobal.com/uk/en/technical-activities/technical-resources-search/2015/march/thresholds.html] FRS 102 section 9.3(e) mirrors the small-group exemption (S, same ACCA page). Pending changes are flagged on legislation.gov.uk (2026 c. 23, Sch 36), effect unknown.
- **Ireland.** Companies Act 2014 s293: a holding company must prepare group statements under the Companies Act (s294) or IFRS (s295); holding companies of the small and micro regimes are exempt (s293(1A)). [P: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2014/act/38/section/293/revised/en/html] Company-level thresholds from financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2024: small is €7.5m balance sheet, €15m turnover, 50 employees; medium €25m, €50m, 250. [S: https://www.charteredaccountants.ie/News/increased-size-limits-for-irish-companies-signed-into-law (via search)] No medium-group exemption found in the s293 text.
- **Nigeria** (CAMA 2020; amendments not checked). Section 379 requires group statements; the FRC exemption and directors' exclusions exist [S: https://lawglobalhub.com/?p=59006]; section 397 allows modified accounts only for a "small group" [S: https://www.lawglobalhub.com/?p=59047]. Small company means turnover up to N120m and net assets up to N60m (Cliffe Dekker via aelex, https://aelex.com/?p=24492); Aelex says N65m, which is a disagreement. IFRS is required for public-interest entities; IFRS for SMEs was "permitted" per a 2016 profile. [P: https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/jurisdictions/pdf-profiles/nigeria-ifrs-profile.pdf]
- **Sierra Leone** (Companies Act 2009). Section 290 requires a consolidated balance sheet and profit and loss; exempt if wholly owned by a Sierra Leone body corporate; directors may exclude subsidiaries on the same four grounds as Nigeria; statements must give a true and fair view and comply with IFRS as adopted by ICASL (ss. 288 to 289, 291); section 304 allows modified group statements only for a small group. [P: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/edocs/lexdocs/laws/en/sl/sl001en.pdf]
- **Inference (researcher 3):** most small-business tenants are legally exempt in the UK and Ireland, so demand comes from voluntary, lender, investor and management reporting, and from medium groups.

### 3.4 How systems implement consolidation
- **NetSuite OneWorld:** an elimination subsidiary holds only elimination journals, in the parent's base currency, outside the general ledger [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268759.html]; consolidated rates Average, Current, Historical [P]; eliminations run from the Period Close Checklist and can be re-run [P: .../bridgehead_N1501792.html]; Automated Intercompany Management flags intercompany sales invoices and vendor bills as elimination candidates [P: .../section_N1486393.html (via search)].
- **Dynamics 365:** a separate consolidation legal entity rerun whenever source entities change; balances persisted, adjustments via closing-period adjustments; elimination journals post in an elimination entity, and daily journals can be posted only there; Financial Reporting offers an on-demand alternative with drill-back to source transactions; Microsoft says the choice depends "on the preferences of their auditors". [P: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/financial-consolidations-currency-translation]
- **Oracle FCCS:** partner elimination uses an Intercompany Partner dimension and a "Partner Eliminations" data source. [P: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financial-consolidation-cloud/toutorial-fcc-partner-elimination/index.html]
- **SAP Group Reporting:** collect, prepare (translate), intercompany match and eliminate, investment eliminations, report. [P: https://learning.sap.com/courses/explaining-corporate-close-with-group-reporting/describing-consolidation-processes_e0a49f1c-8d52-4a07-94dd-b1e932b46b5e]
- **Odoo 19:** account mapping codes and separate consolidation adjustment journals visible only in a multi-ledger; equity at historical rate, profit and loss at weighted average, balance sheet at closing rate. [P: Odoo 19 consolidation page above] A forum user says the docs do not explain intercompany eliminations and that the old Consolidation app was removed in v18 (S: https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/consolidations-within-odoo-version-19-288028).
- **Xero and QuickBooks:** Xero says native consolidation is not planned and points to Syft, Fathom or Joiin (S: Xero forum reply https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967133-reports-tax/suggestions/44960413-reporting-ability-to-consolidate-multiple-xero-o); Syft claims eliminations and 170+ currencies; Fathom says it does not natively support partial ownership (S: https://www.fathomhq.com/blog/the-best-financial-consolidation-software-solutions); QuickBooks Online has no native consolidation, its Spreadsheet Sync merges only identically named accounts and does no eliminations or translation (S: https://www.liveflow.com/learn/can-quickbooks-do-consolidated-financial-statements).
- **Reporting layer versus consolidation ledger.** Reporting layer on demand: nothing persisted, so no elimination journals to audit; reproducibility depends on freezing rates and mappings; a Syft reviewer notes no record of the FX rates used (S). Consolidation ledger: eliminations and adjustments are posted journals with their own audit trail (NetSuite offers an Intercompany Elimination audit report, P: .../section_N1498982.html); the cost is reruns and stale-data risk after source changes (Dynamics, P). **Hybrid (inference):** compute on demand, persist a frozen run with its inputs.

### 3.5 Intercompany
- Counterparty tag: Dynamics uses a "Trading Partner" dimension or dedicated accounts [P]; FCCS uses the ICP dimension [P]; NetSuite pairs intercompany transactions (status Linked) [P].
- Mismatch detection: NetSuite's reconciliation report finds unlinked orders and mismatched amounts, quantities and billing [P: .../section_N1502183.html]; SAP uses reason codes to split translation difference from other difference (S, vendor blog https://blogs.sap.com/2020/09/30/matching-reconciliation-elimination/, from search snippet).
- Timing: NetSuite reverses flagged journal lines automatically next period. [P]
- **UK recharges** (S: https://www.rsmuk.com/insights/tax-voice/valuation-of-management-charges-for-vat-purposes ; https://mha.co.uk/insights/the-complexities-of-vat-and-intercompany-recharges): management charges between separate VAT-registered companies need VAT; group recharges are valued on cost for VAT; HMRC may challenge them under transfer pricing; document the basis in an agreement.

### 3.6 Chart mapping and close order
- Dynamics options [P]: keep the source accounts, set a default consolidation account per source account, or use consolidation account groups. Odoo maps by shared code [P].
- Order: SAP prepare and translate, then match and eliminate, then investment eliminations [P]; NetSuite runs elimination only after the other close tasks [P]; FCCS order (translate, match, eliminate, non-controlling interest, adjustments) is third-party only [S: https://www.hyperbots.com/glossary/fccs-consolidation].

### 3.7 Researcher 3's minimal design (inference, not sourced)
Build first: a per-entity close lock; a group chart-mapping table; translation by rate type (closing, average, historical for equity) with a translation-reserve line; a partner tag on intercompany lines; an intercompany reconciliation report; elimination journals in a non-posting consolidation scope, persisted per run with frozen rates; consolidated trial balance, balance sheet and income statement with drill-down. Defer: non-controlling interest, goodwill, equity method, consolidated cash flow, unrealised profit in stock, multi-level hierarchies. Main risks: translation residual not balancing, different period-ends, mapping drift, rate-source disputes, presenting output as statutory accounts when exempt or non-IFRS entities are involved, pending UK legislative changes.

## 5. Where sources disagree or are weak (read before relying)

1. **Intragroup monetary FX on consolidation.** The IFRIC paper (P) keeps the exchange difference on an eliminated intragroup monetary item in consolidated profit or loss (para 45); NetSuite's worked example (P) puts the elimination residual in a CTA-E account. Researcher 3's own understanding, unsourced, is that para 32 sends it to OCI only if the item is part of the net investment. **The vendor behaviour must be tested against IAS 21 before FiSH copies it.**
2. **QuickBooks Online balance sheet by class.** Intuit's own pages conflict (an article that says there is no true balance sheet by Class, a related-articles list that still names one, and a Desktop article describing one).
3. **Nigeria small-company thresholds:** N120m turnover and N60m net assets (Cliffe Dekker) versus N65m (Aelex).
4. **Nigeria parallel-market gap:** 24 to 57 naira per dollar by outlet and date; no authoritative reconciliation.
5. **IAS 21 and IFRS 10 paragraph numbers** and all quoted behaviour: unconfirmed against the licensed text; confirm before any requirement cites a paragraph.
6. **Rate-source gap for FiSH's own currencies:** neither the Bank of England nor the ECB publishes NGN or SLE, and the central-bank sources for the Naira and the Leone have no confirmed machine-readable feed or reuse terms (section 1.4). This is a finding about the rate table, not a research failure.
7. **Weak coverage:** nothing found on backdated rates, mixed-currency totals, foreign bank reconciliation design, effective-dated dimension structures, or a departmental balance sheet.
